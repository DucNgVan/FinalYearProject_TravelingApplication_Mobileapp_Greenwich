# 03. Project Vision & Value Proposition

## Nomadix — All-in-one Smart Travel Platform
**Final Year Project (FYP) — University of Greenwich**  
**Student:** Nguyen Van Duc  

---

## 1. Project Vision

> **"To create an integrated travel platform where travelers and companion groups can discover destinations, compare travel options, co-plan their journeys in real time, transparently manage group expenses, experience local culture, verify physical visits, earn achievements, and contribute trusted travel knowledge to the community."**

---

## 2. Project Mission

> **"To simplify independent and group travel by combining travel discovery, collaborative multi-user planning, shared expense management, location-based experiences, gamification, and verified community knowledge into a single mobile platform."**

## 4. Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

> **Location-verified travel experience combined with gamification and community credibility.**

Nomadix establishes an unbroken feedback loop connecting real-world travel to digital community authority:

```text
User visits Da Nang
        ↓
GPS verifies physical location (< 100m geofence)
        ↓
Landmark check-in unlocked
        ↓
Photo captured & uploaded to cloud
        ↓
Cultural quiz completed
        ↓
XP awarded & Level progressed
        ↓
"Da Nang Explorer" Badge unlocked
        ↓
User answers Da Nang questions in Community Q&A
        ↓
System displays "Da Nang Verified" trust indicator badge
```

> **Verification Philosophy:** The "City Verified" indicator certifies that the contributor has demonstrated physical presence and cultural engagement at that destination, creating a higher standard of authentic community trust.
